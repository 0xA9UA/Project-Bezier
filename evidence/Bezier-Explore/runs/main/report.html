Bezier-Explore results

Bezier-Explore: measured results

Completed and independently reloaded all 72 trained checkpoints on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090. Nine architectures, four tasks and two seeds (7 and 19) use a frozen training plan. All test metrics below are from validation-selected checkpoints. No test scores selected training settings.

The study distinguishes three questions: whether sharing reduces storage, whether the curve improves quality at that size, and whether a restricted analytic field can replace numerical integration. A positive result for one does not establish the others.

Read the concise findings for the interpretation, or the second-width recall comparison for the separately frozen 18-run capacity supplement.

Restricted analytic integration

The polynomial coefficient field has total spatial degree at most 3. Along a cubic route it has time degree at most 9, so five-point Gauss-Legendre integration is exact in real arithmetic. Stored FP32 coefficients and arithmetic have rounding error. The hidden-state formula also requires a shared orthonormal basis and commuting linear field operators; it is not a general neural ODE solver.

The following compares the same trained analytic Bézier model with RK4 in the same shared basis. It reports the smallest tested RK4 step count reaching relative output L2 error ≤1e-5 on the first 256 test examples. This numerical tolerance is not an accuracy matching criterion between different architectures.

Reversing the entire control-point order leaves the commuting analytic flow unchanged in real arithmetic. Across its trained models, the largest observed FP32 output difference under reversal was 1.14e-05. This symmetry is a restriction as well as a possible useful invariant; the nonlinear field is not required to share it.

Runtime, precision and verification

GPU timings are synchronized end-to-end eager forward passes with device-resident inputs. The frozen initial protocol uses 15 warmups and 5 repeats of 60 iterations; those measurements remain in each evaluation.json. Because other GPU workloads were observed, this report uses an additional remeasurement: three rounds with randomized model/batch ordering, 15 warmups and 40 timed calls per entry, taking each run's median. The report averages the two seed medians. The numerical solver comparison uses its separate 3-repeat measurements. Host preprocessing, transfer and process startup are excluded. Interleaving reduces ordering effects but cannot remove desktop or concurrent-process contention. See timing_recheck.json and evaluation_environment.json for observed processes.

Every checkpoint was independently reloaded and its test predictions checked against the saved prediction artifact. Scientific source hashes and dataset hashes are frozen in plan.json. Mathematical unit tests check curve containment, route gradients, quadrature exactness, orthonormal retraction and agreement with RK4. FP16 size represents stored weights round-tripped into FP32 computation; it is not a claim about FP16 throughput.

Limits and next decision

The experiment covers one hidden width, one rank, one training schedule and two seeds. It does not establish asymptotic scaling, an advantage on large language models, or a universal storage/reliability benefit. Parameter sharing and commuting dynamics restrict the function class; a smaller model can lose recall or generalization. For arbitrary independent 128-way labels, the label information requirement is 7 bits per fact: 114,688 bits (14 KiB) for 16,384 facts, before storing keys or a decoder. This is a bound for arbitrary associations, not a claim that our seeded synthetic data are literally incompressible: a specialized program supplied their generator and seed could regenerate them. The tested neural models receive neither. A finite-precision curve does not eliminate the information requirement for arbitrary exact associations.

For a follow-up, compare quality-matched size sweeps and optimized kernels, then test genuine language or domain knowledge. Select a candidate because its measured quality/size/time tradeoff is useful, not because a route looks geometrically compact.
